Frequently asked questions

Common questions about it consulting

Do you also implement the recommendations, or just advise?

Either. Some clients want the roadmap and handle execution themselves; others want us to implement it directly.

How long does a technology audit take?

Most audits for a small business take one to two weeks, depending on how many systems and locations are involved.

Are you affiliated with any hardware or software vendors?

No. Recommendations aren't tied to reseller commissions, which is part of why the advice tends to differ from a typical vendor-based consultant.

Is this a one-time engagement or ongoing?

Both models exist. Many clients start with a one-time audit and roadmap, then move to periodic check-ins.

Do you work with businesses that don't have any in-house IT?

That's the most common client. Fractional leadership fills exactly that gap.
